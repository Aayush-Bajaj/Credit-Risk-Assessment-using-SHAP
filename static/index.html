FAQ

Common questions

What does the percentage mean?

It is the estimated probability that the applicant will default on the loan.

What is the threshold?

The cut-off tuned for the model. Scores at or above it are flagged as high risk.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a model estimate and should support, not replace, human judgement.
